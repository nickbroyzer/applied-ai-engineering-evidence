**D319 — Consent is stamped at the tap, never at mount (LOCKED, Session 403).** The checkout screen creates its claim on mount, so any consent timestamp written at claim creation records a consent not yet given. disclosure_version is recorded at creation (which wording was shown); disclosure_accepted_at is written only by the picker-only, idempotent accept-disclosure endpoint, called at the Place-hold tap before payment confirmation, refused once the claim leaves pending_payment. Payment is blocked if the acceptance call fails. Device-proven: 13.1s gap between claim creation and consent on the verification walk, against 0.09s under the old behavior.

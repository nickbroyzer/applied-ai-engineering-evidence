**D212 — Authorization Expiry Protection**

STATUS: LOCKED (product) — implementation pending, NET-NEW. RAG: internal. Fixes a live money-leak.

PROBLEM (verified this session): the $2 hold is created with capture_method="manual" and no expiry handling (stripe_service.py:126, :155). A Stripe card hold is cancelled 7 days after creation BY DEFAULT, but the window varies by card brand — as short as 5 days on Visa (Stripe docs, verified). The 7-day clock starts at claim time. Auto-capture is timed off pickup: geofence → pickup +2h, no geofence → pickup +24h. The pickup-window validator allows a pickup up to 6 days after claim (_validate_pickup_window, _PICKUP_WINDOW_DAYS). A pickup booked on day 6 + the 24h no-geofence wait lands capture on day 7+, after the hold has already been auto-voided by Stripe — so a no-show in that window collects $0. The 6-day auto-cancel (D105) does NOT cover this: it only cancels claims with no pickup coordination; a scheduled pickup rides the pickup-time math. Net safe booking horizon on Visa is only ~4 days (5-day hold minus the 24h capture wait minus margin).

DECISION (three parts, all net-new):
(1) Tighten the pickup booking window from 6 days to 4 days so capture always lands while the hold is alive on every card brand, even with no backstop.
(2) Store the real per-hold expiry date on the claim at hold-creation time, read from Stripe's capture_before field on the charge (available on standard manual-capture holds; no extended-authorization feature required — verified Stripe docs). Stripe instructs relying on capture_before rather than assuming 7 days.
(3) Add a backstop to the existing sweeper: before a hold's stored expiry, force charge-or-cancel so no hold ever expires uncollected.

Also correct the stale "7-day auth window is Stripe's default" comment at stripe_service.py:126 as part of this work — the assumption is wrong for Visa.

Amended Session 332: the premise above is false. The 5-day Visa figure applies only to merchant-initiated transactions, where the cardholder is not present. A Zelbi picker taps Claim and authorizes the card himself, which is customer-initiated: the card-not-present authorization window is 7 days on Visa, Mastercard, American Express, and Discover alike. Verified against Stripe's authorization-validity tables and confirmed empirically — a live hold returned a 168-hour window. The real risk was never brand-specific: a day-6 booking plus the 24h no-geofence capture wait lands capture at day 7, the exact hour a 7-day hold auto-voids, with zero margin. Part 1 (the 6→4 day tightening, `ae32bbb`) rested entirely on the false figure and was reverted to 6 days in `7677744`. Part 2 is BUILT (`9276f79`) — the authorization webhook now writes the per-hold expiry read from Stripe's capture_before. Part 3 remains unbuilt and is now the only thing standing between the platform and an uncollected hold; it is cancel-only per the Session 332 decision, never capture-before-pickup. D212 status remains LOCKED; parts 2 and 3 stand unchanged; part 1 is RETIRED.

**D306 (LOCKED Session 398)** — The Zelbi checkout screen's route and copy are settled, and D305's technical premises are both corrected. A full read of the pinned Stripe SDK 0.50.3 established that FlowController, which D305 names as the chosen route, does not exist in the package under that name anywhere — source, functions module, or the useStripe hook. The mechanism is `customFlow`, a boolean on the PaymentSheet configuration that separates method selection from confirmation and requires the caller to run `confirmPaymentSheetPayment`, which the app already holds; the native iOS side honours it at three sites. D305's second premise is also false: the embedded Payment Element was rejected because a callable component was unconfirmed, and it in fact ships `useEmbeddedPaymentElement`, re-exported through the package index with complete native iOS support on both architectures. Both routes were mocked and Nick ruled route A — Stripe's picker opening from a single method row on a Zelbi-owned screen, the app confirming on its own screen — over the embedded route, whose method list Stripe draws inside our layout. The mockup was approved section by section, and the disclosure wording that gate g1 exists to fix was approved and filed verbatim at `docs/agents/zelbi-document-requirements.md` section 5.1. Nick's rulings on that copy: state the commitment rather than deny the charge, so a "not a charge" construction is banned; the post-capture no-refund statement and the givers-never-pay statement are policy-page material and do not belong in front of a picker mid-claim; and the copy describes the finished platform, naming pickup completion and the confirmation photo even though neither path is wired today — which makes the completion wiring a launch blocker for this screen's truthfulness, not a blocker on the build. Section 5.1 additionally binds the build to record consent, not merely display it: the accepted wording's version identifier and the acceptance timestamp are stored against the claim. Neither the consent line nor its storage is in the approved mockup, and both are owed before the screen can carry g1.
**Status: LOCKED**

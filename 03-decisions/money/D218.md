**D218 — Pickup-booking ceiling is a duration from authorization, not a calendar-day count.** A giver may propose or set a pickup time no later than the authorization timestamp plus 5 days 12 hours. The ceiling is derived: the Stripe hold lives 7 days from authorization (customer-initiated, all four card networks per the D212 correction); auto-capture on an unconfirmed arrival waits 24 hours after the pickup time; a 12-hour cushion covers the sweep margin and clock skew. 7 days − 24h − 12h = 5 days 12 hours. This guarantees a no-geofence capture always lands while the hold is still alive, with no backstop sweep required. Enforced in the booking validator. Supersedes the flat 6-day window. The giver never sees a partial-day cutoff — day rows past the ceiling simply do not render. Filed Session 333. LOCKED.

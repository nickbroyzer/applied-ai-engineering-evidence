D627 — LOCKED (2026-10-27, Nick's ruling from a phone test on build 18). CARD OR BANNER, NEVER BOTH. PARTIAL REVERSAL OF D625. The live card and the banner must never both fire for the same moment. When the person's card is up, the card is the alert (it pings) and no banner is sent; the banner is only the backup, sent when no card will show the moment. Reverses D625 points (4) and (5): the banner always for people whose push reaches them, and their quiet card update. Evidence: on the device a swipe reached the server within about one second with Zelbi force-quit (dismissed_at 17:23:23, update token cleared, no app run since 17:09:43), so the server can know a card is gone. Built: (1) card_carries is True when, for one of the person's cards of the moment's kinds, the row is not fallen back, the install is this backend's app, not revoked, Live Activities on, and EITHER the card is open, the phone has not reported it dismissed or ended, and it has its update token or its start or restart was accepted by Apple within TOKEN_GRACE (5 minutes, counted from the latest start or restart), OR it ended showing the completion (end_reason complete, not fallen back). D625's "confirmed alive within 30 minutes" and "no push can reach the person" are removed (ALIVE_WINDOW deleted; confirmed_alive_at stays as a record). (2) Every alerting card update carries the card's alert again for every person (Time to go, You're here / They're here, Did you pick it up?, the completion for the person who did not tap it); the start keeps its alert. (3) A card the phone no longer has is push-started at every alerting moment as D625 built (cap 4 starts), and a restart Apple accepted in the same sync carries the moment, so its banner is held back; a refused restart falls back and the banner goes. (4) Order at each banner site: arrival (claims.confirm_geofence), completion (confirm_pickup, collect_answer, post_window_sweeper auto-capture) and card 11 (post_window_sweeper collect ask, which now passes its own clock to card_carries) already synced the cards (nudge) before deciding. The pickup-time push (pickup_reminder, a plus-or-minus 5-minute band on a 10-minute job) could decide before the card's own Did you pick it up? at the pickup time: a picker with a pickup card (a card-capable install, or a card row on the claim) is now decided at the first run at or after the pickup time, after a nudge, found up to 15 minutes after it, once (notification_idempotency); pickers without a card keep the band exactly. (5) A card without its update token that reaches a moment with a push of its own falls back at once, so that push goes; at a moment without one (Time to go, the giver's meet-up They're here) it waits out TOKEN_GRACE for its token. (6) A card taken back by a late token (late_token) jumps to its stage without an alert: the moments it missed went as notifications. (7) A completion on a card swiped away beforehand now falls back (it was left un-fallen-back under e4 A), so its push goes. People whose notifications are off keep the card ping; fallback_route and the email rules are unchanged; no notification copy and no email type was added; no migration. Left for Nick: a giver who earns a giveaway credit at completion still gets the card ping and the credit banner (the card does not say a credit was earned); Time to go (both sides) and the giver's meet-up They're here have no push, so a person whose card cannot show them gets nothing; a person who completed the pickup themselves sees the card's completion without an alert and no banner (e3 A).

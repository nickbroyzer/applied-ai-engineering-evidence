D629 — LOCKED (2026-10-27, Nick's ruling R1). ARRIVAL STAMPS ONLY AT 25 M ON A 20 M READING; REGION WAKES ARE SILENT. "You're here" stamps only when one GPS reading puts the phone within 25 m of the item AND that reading's own accuracy is 20 m or better. The 150 m region only wakes the app; a wake on it that does not stamp registers a 100 m region for one more wake, unless the measured distance is already under 100 m (iOS sends no entry for a region the phone is already in), in which case watching stops; a 100 m wake that does not stamp stops watching. Wakes are silent: no card change, no banner, no new copy. The hub's in-app check uses the same rule; the manual I'm here is unchanged; no coordinates reach the server. Built: mobile/utils/arrivalRule.ts (pure: the constants 25 m, 20 m, 150 m, 100 m, 8 s, 15 s; takeFix, readArrival, afterWake) with utils/arrivalRule.test.mjs (13 cases, plain Node); mobile/utils/arrivalWatcher.ts checkArrival, shared by the region task and the hub: a fresh expo-location fix at Accuracy.Highest raced against 8 s, else getLastKnownPositionAsync only if under 15 s old and 20 m or better, then an arrival_check event (D628) and confirmGeofence only on the rule. The region id carries its ring as a sixth part; ids an older build registered read as the 150 m ring. Both ARRIVAL_RADIUS_M = 150 constants are gone. The window and 60 s registration-grace checks are kept. No background mode was added: expo-location 19.0.8's getCurrentPositionAsync checks only the foreground permission and makes one CLLocationManager requestLocation() call with allowsBackgroundLocationUpdates false (source read; device proof owed). Left for Nick: the grace also applies to the 100 m ring, so a 100 m entry within 60 s of the 150 m wake (about 50 m of walking) is dropped; a wake with no usable fix is tracked with null distance and accuracy and, on the 150 m ring, still watches the 100 m ring. Needs D628 deployed before the build ships. Commit: this commit.

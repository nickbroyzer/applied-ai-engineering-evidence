(1) POST /knowledge/ask requires a signed-in account (it previously answered anyone who reached the server's address), limited to 30 questions an hour per account. (2) At most 1,000 questions a day (UTC) are sent to Voyage, counted from knowledge_query_logs so restarts and multiple servers cannot reset it; past that, search falls back to free text-only search until midnight UTC, and help never stops answering. (3) Questions are capped at 300 characters on the server and in the app. (4) API-abuse alerts are written to watcher_events (family "api", source "zelbi"): any account hitting any per-account rate limit (help, uploads, AI), at most one row per account per route per hour; and the platform passing 80% and 100% of the daily Voyage ceiling, once each per UTC day. (5) Voyage's own Budget Limits alert is set at $2 a month (email only; it does not stop requests). The Control Center does not yet read watcher_events, so these alerts are stored but not yet shown.

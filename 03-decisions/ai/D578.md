D578 — LOCKED (2026-10-07, Nick's rulings on the plan "Support agent on reports: revision 4", Session 476). SUPPORT AGENT ON REPORTS, REVISION 4. Amends D569, D570, D572 and D577. Spec: docs/agents/agent-foundation-spec.md, "Revision 4 (2026-10-07), approved by Nick: current plan"; revision 3 below it is superseded where revision 4 says so.
(1) Scope: the agent works complaints (Report a problem records) only. Disputes join when a route creates dispute records (D574). Rule flags stay out (D577).
(2) Reads: the report; the listing and its photos; the pickup (timeline, arrival, photos) and the messages between the two people; both people's past reports and decisions; both people's account standing.
(3) Decisions: hide the listing, pause the person, hide and pause, no action, or suspend (person reports: pause, no action or suspend). Each comes with a one-line reason. The agent can suggest suspension but never carry it out.
(4) Approval happens in the Decision box of Zelbi Pulse Reports: Approve (it runs), Change (Nick picks another option), Reject (the report stays open).
(5) Hour 23: a report still open 23 hours after it was sent is decided by the agent using undoable actions only (hide the listing, pause the person, no action). It does not repeat an action Nick rejected. Suspension always waits for Nick.
(6) Deadline alerts always on: the hour-20 and hour-23 alerts for open and held reports involve no AI and run regardless of the master switch and the support mode. Only agent work (runs, model calls, actions) is gated by the switches.
(7) Alerts reach Nick by push and by email to [email], the one exception to "[that address] gets no automated mail". The push goes to admin push devices (a registration that survives sign-out and other accounts) as well as the admin account's own token.
(8) Cost: $3.00 a day by default, changed from Zelbi Pulse (Health and settings, Agents). At the cap the agent stops until UTC midnight; low Anthropic credit hands off and alerts.
(9) Paused reporters: a report held because its reporter's account is reporting-locked is never worked by the agent, even after the lock is cleared. Nick still gets its hour-20 and hour-23 alerts.
(10) No money, ever: no refunds, charges or Stripe calls, and no email to test accounts.
(11) Model: Haiku 5.5 (claude-haiku-5-5) by default, with a three-model eval (Haiku 5.5, Haiku 4.5, Sonnet 5.5); Auto only after Nick has seen the scores (D572 amendment).
(12) Evidence goes in the public repo [this repository], as a synthetic offline package, write-up, decision records and recorded scores; docs/engineering-evidence/ in zelbi stays the private working copy.
(13) Build order: groups 1-14 in backlog rows "Support agent G1" to "Support agent G14"; the revision 3 group rows are superseded.

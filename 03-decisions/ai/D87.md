**D87 — AI listing-generation brand identification hallucination** *(PROPOSED Session 181)*

AI listing generation produces plausible-sounding but incorrect brand names for product photos. Test case (Session 180): Geek Bar vape photos generated as "Freemax Pulse X," "Vandy Vape Pulse," "Pulse X Disposable" — all wrong; actual brand on packaging is Geek Bar. Suggests the prompt is asking the model to guess brand from visual style rather than read visible branding on packaging. Give-flow trust and scam-risk implications — wrong product names mislead pickers and erode trust scores; related to D32 stricter AI scam detection on first listing. Phase 8D AI accuracy scope alongside D82–D85 model selection benchmark + cost unit economics. Triage scope: (a) verify prompt currently directs model to read visible branding, (b) confirm photo resolution is sufficient for text recognition on packaging, (c) decide whether brand identification belongs as a separate prompted field with its own confidence score, (d) decide whether D32 should extend to brand-confidence flagging.

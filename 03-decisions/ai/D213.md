**D213 (LOCKED S329):** Prohibited items policy. MVP enforcement is honor-system + reporting — no server-side prohibited list, no automated blocking at launch; the report path handles violations. Prohibited: weapons/ammo; illegal drugs, controlled substances, prescription medication; alcohol, tobacco, vapes; supplements and opened personal-care products; hazardous/recalled/expired/unsafe items; fireworks, chemicals, explosives; live animals; stolen or counterfeit goods; sexual products or explicit adult material; items carrying personal or financial data (IDs, cards, mail, tax documents, hard drives, phones, laptops, storage devices unless fully wiped); gift cards, cash, tickets, or monetary instruments; vehicles or anything requiring title, registration, or regulated transfer; homemade, cooked, or perishable food; used mattresses/pillows or items with pests, mold, bodily fluids, or contamination; services, rentals, or non-item offers; hate, extremist, or exploitative material. Allowed: unopened, shelf-stable, commercially packaged food.

Public copy ("What you can't give away on Zelbi"): "Zelbi is for safe, legal, everyday items you would feel comfortable handing to a neighbor. Please don't list weapons or ammo; drugs, prescription medication, alcohol, tobacco, or vapes; hazardous, recalled, expired, or unsafe items; live animals; stolen or counterfeit goods; sexual products or explicit adult material; gift cards, cash, tickets, or anything mainly used like money; vehicles or anything requiring title or registration; homemade, cooked, or perishable food; used mattresses or items with pests, mold, bodily fluids, or other contamination; services, rentals, or non-item offers; or anything containing someone's personal or financial information. Unopened, shelf-stable packaged food is okay. If you're not sure an item is safe to hand over in person, leave it off Zelbi. If you see something that breaks these rules, report it and we'll review it."

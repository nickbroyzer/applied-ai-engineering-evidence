**D272 (LOCKED Session 377)** — No listing is ever hidden at post time. `create_listing` previously set any listing whose scam checks returned a signal to `hidden_pending_review`, silently: the giver saw a success screen, the listing never reached Discover, and nothing told them. D265 framed the fix as making that suppression visible. Nick rejected that framing and the entry it produced: a notification saying "your listing is under review" is still a listing nobody can claim, it delays a move-out giver who needs the item gone, and — the case that settles it — a listing with a short deadline burns its whole window while hidden and can expire before anyone ever sees it. At launch scale each acquired user is expensive and a single bad first impression is unrecoverable, which is the same reasoning that deleted rules 1 and 2 in D264 and D270. The two hiding lines were removed; `write_auto_flag` is untouched, so every trip still writes a report row and the evidence trail for the future moderation dashboard accumulates from now. The `hidden_pending_review` status remains legal in the schema and the server still computes an `under_review` flag on several listing responses — both are now unreachable from listing creation and were deliberately left in place rather than ripped out mid-session. Zero rows were in that status at close. Two wiring tests that asserted hiding were rewritten to assert the listing stays active, three tests for the deleted rule 1 and the multi-rule case were removed, and the L-6 feed test was inverted to prove a flagged listing still reaches pickers. `test_scam_wiring.py` and `test_scam_detection.py` are 62 passed. Consequence to hold: D265 is closed by this entry, not built. Commit `fa9c9bb`.

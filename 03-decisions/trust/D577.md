D577 — LOCKED (2026-10-06, Nick's rulings through live mockups, Session 476). REPORT A PROBLEM AND THE SUPPORT AGENT'S SCOPE. Amends D569, D570 and D574.
(1) One way to report: the Report a problem screen. Its email path is removed. Three ways in: Settings > Report a problem; the Handoff ••• menu's Report a problem; and "Report listing" as an action inside the listing's share sheet (Zelbi's own share sheet, mobile/modules/zelbi-share), shown on other people's listings only. No new icon is added to the listing header; the owner keeps Remove listing.
(2) Layout: when the screen opens from a listing (share-sheet action or Handoff menu), that listing sits on top under "Listing you're reporting", and the person's other listings (given or claimed, newest first) sit below under "Your listings". Opened from Settings, only "Your listings" shows.
(3) A listing the person has no part in (not theirs, not claimed) goes straight to listing reasons: Offensive language, Inappropriate photo or content, Harmful or dangerous item, Looks like a scam, Something else. A listing they claimed asks "The person" or "The item" first. Item reasons are the same five. Person reasons for someone who claimed: Didn't show up, Rude or threatening, Asked to pay outside Zelbi, Tried to move the chat off Zelbi, Something else. For someone who gave the item, Didn't show up is left out (the hub's "Was your item picked up?" card already reports a no-show). Open: whether a listing the person gave skips "person or item" and goes straight to person reasons. Only Something else opens one optional line of text.
(4) After sending: "Thanks, we'll look into it". A reported listing is hidden for the reporter everywhere (Discover, map, Saved, search) and cannot be reported twice; an active pickup on it stays in Handoffs until it ends.
(5) Report limits: the 3rd report within an hour or the 10th in a day goes through, then reporting is locked for that account (everything else in the app still works); the account goes to the Control Center for Nick; reports from a locked account wait and are not worked by the agent until he clears it. Locked message: "You've sent several reports, so we'll be reviewing them before you can report anymore."
(6) Reports land in a Report a problem section of the Control Center. The support agent works complaints (these reports) and disputes. Rule flags are removed from the agent's scope; the message guards keep running and keep writing their records.
(7) Timing (amends D570 for complaints): the agent proposes; Nick has 23 hours to approve or reject; if he has not answered, the agent carries out on its own only actions that can be undone (hide the listing; pause the person's posting and claiming). Permanent actions (suspension, outright deletion) always wait for Nick. Reason: Apple's rejection letters under guideline 1.2 require objectionable content to be acted on within 24 hours.
(8) Agent daily cost cap stays $3.00 per agent for launch (D572), to be raised as usage grows.
(Nick-attested.) Commit: this commit.
D577 amended (2026-10-06, Session 476, Nick-attested):
(a) a listing the person gave goes straight to the person reasons;
(b) a picker's "Didn't show up" only files a complaint, the claim fee is still charged by the existing rules and a dispute that goes the picker's way is refunded, with no change to charging;
(c) "Your listings" shows only listings where another person was involved;
(d) the three other email paths (Ask Zelbi Help, the receipt's Contact support, the suspended-account appeal) stay email; listings the person claimed get the item reason "Not there or not as described", which is how money disputes come in;
(e) limits are 3 in an hour or 5 in a day, replacing 3 and 10, and every complaint from a locked account waits for Nick, including the one that tripped the lock;
(f) the 23-hour clock is decided when the agent is built;
(g) "pause" is a new hold that stops a reported person from posting and claiming until lifted, set from the Control Center, built with that section, its on-screen message approved by Nick.

**D215 (LOCKED S329):** Account deletion / data policy. MVP behavior is real in-app soft-delete. The delete-account element already exists in Settings under Log out as plain red text but is a front-end no-op — the backend delete endpoint is net-new. On confirm, the endpoint marks the account deleted, signs the user out immediately, blocks re-login, and anonymizes the public identity (display name → "Deleted user", avatar and bio cleared, email dissociated from the profile). Active claims are cancelled and open card holds voided as part of deletion. Records required for integrity — completed pickups, disputes, block/report history — are retained in de-identified form and never shown publicly. No grace period and no undo for MVP; deletion is immediate and permanent from the user's side. Hard-delete was considered and rejected for MVP (breaks claim/dispute/payment record links, higher build and correctness risk); soft-delete with anonymization is App-Store-safe, since Apple permits retaining records required for legal or financial reasons.

UI direction (Nick, S329): the Delete account control lives in Settings directly under the Log out button and is restyled to match Log out — same white card treatment — with red "Delete Account" label text.

User-facing copy: "This permanently deletes your Zelbi account and removes your profile, listings, and saved items. You'll be signed out and won't be able to log back in. Some records of completed pickups and disputes may be kept as required for safety and legal reasons. This can't be undone."

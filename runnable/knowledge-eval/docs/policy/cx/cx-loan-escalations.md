# Handling escalated loan disputes

This document is SYNTHETIC. It stands in for a real tier-CX document: staff
guidance that a support agent may read and a member may not. It is reachable
from the CX index and structurally unreachable from the public index.

## When to escalate a damage assessment

Escalate when the member disputes the branch assessment in writing, or when
the same tool has been assessed as damaged on two consecutive loans. Do not
escalate on a first verbal disagreement — resolve it at the branch first.

## Who decides a disputed assessment

A regional coordinator decides, not the branch that made the original
assessment. The coordinator reads the branch note and the member's account of
events before ruling. The ruling is recorded on the loan, and the member is
told the outcome and the reason.

## Reinstating a suspended borrower account

A borrower account suspended after an escalation is reinstated by the
coordinator who ruled, never by the branch. Reinstatement is recorded with the
reason so a later reviewer can see why it was granted.

## What to tell a member while an escalation is open

Confirm that the escalation is open and that a coordinator will decide.
Do not predict the outcome and do not describe the internal criteria the
coordinator applies.

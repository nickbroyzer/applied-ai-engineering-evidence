# Borrowing a tool from the library

This document is SYNTHETIC. It exists so this package runs end to end without
the real policy corpus. It is written in the same shape as a real tier-public
document — frontmatter contract, H2 sections, plain member-facing prose — but
it describes an invented tool-lending library, not any real product.

## What a loan hold is

When you reserve a tool, the library places a **hold** on your borrower
account. A hold is not a charge and no money moves. It reserves the tool in
your name so nobody else can reserve the same one while you are on your way.
The hold is released automatically once the tool comes back.

A hold is never a deposit and never a membership fee. Nothing is deducted from
your account when a hold is placed.

## How long a reservation lasts

A reservation lasts until the pickup window you chose ends. If you do not
collect the tool inside that window, the reservation lapses on its own and the
hold is released. You are free to reserve the same tool again afterwards.

## Returning a tool

Bring the tool back to the same branch you collected it from. A staff member
confirms the return, and the hold on your account is released at that moment.
If the branch is closed, use the after-hours return slot and the hold is
released when staff process the slot the next working morning.

## Late returns

A tool returned after its due date is a late return. The library records the
late return on your borrower account and the hold is released as normal. Late
returns affect how many tools you may reserve at once.

## If a tool is damaged

Tell a staff member when you return it. Damage is assessed at the branch, and
you will be told the outcome before anything is recorded against your account.
Do not attempt a repair yourself — an attempted repair usually makes the
assessment worse, not better.

## Lost tools

Report a lost tool as soon as you know. The library will tell you what happens
next. Reporting it yourself is always better than waiting for the due date to
pass.

## Contacting the library

For anything this page does not cover, email the library at
support@zelbi.ai and a member of staff will help.
